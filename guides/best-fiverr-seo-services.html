What SEO work is worth outsourcing?

  • Technical SEO audits with a clear issue list and recommendations.
  • On-page optimization for existing pages.
  • Keyword and topic research tied to real search intent.
  • Content briefs that give writers useful search and user guidance.
  • Site migrations, redirects and structured technical cleanup.

What to avoid

Be cautious with services promising guaranteed Google rankings, huge numbers of backlinks, automated traffic or instant authority. SEO outcomes depend on competition, site quality, content, links and search-engine changes.

How to choose a freelancer

Look for a specific scope.
Prefer an audit, page optimization package or defined research deliverable over a generic “SEO boost.” Review samples, understand exactly what will be delivered, and avoid giving a freelancer access they do not need.

Start small

For a new site, a small audit or one-page optimization project is often a better first experiment than a large recurring package. Measure what changed and whether organic impressions, rankings, clicks or conversions improve.
