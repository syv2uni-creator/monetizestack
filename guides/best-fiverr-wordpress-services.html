Useful WordPress tasks to outsource

  • Theme and plugin troubleshooting.
  • Page speed and performance optimization.
  • Responsive layout fixes.
  • Small customizations and CSS changes.
  • WordPress migrations and setup.
  • Security and maintenance tasks with a clearly defined scope.

How to avoid a bad project

Describe the exact problem, WordPress version and relevant theme or plugin details. Ask what access is required and keep backups before granting access. Avoid vague promises such as “make my website perfect” and prefer measurable deliverables.

Best first project: give a freelancer one contained issue. A successful small job gives you evidence about communication, technical quality and reliability before you commit to more work.

Questions to ask before ordering

  • What exactly is included?
  • What information or access do you need?
  • Will you work on a staging site where possible?
  • How will the finished result be tested?
  • What happens if the issue is caused by a plugin conflict?
