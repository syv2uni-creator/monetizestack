Affiliate Tools for Website Monetization

For a new website with a €0 budget, affiliate marketing is worth testing before relying on display advertising. The goal is not to promote everything; it is to recommend products that naturally solve problems for readers who are already trying to build, grow or monetize a website.

Strategy: start with a small number of relevant programs, publish genuinely useful comparison and tutorial content, disclose affiliate relationships clearly, and measure clicks before expanding.

Our first shortlist

1. Webflow

Good fit for content about building websites, landing pages and online businesses. Webflow says its affiliate program pays 50% of a qualified new user's first Site or Workspace subscription, with monthly-plan commissions available for up to 12 months or one annual-plan purchase.

Official Webflow affiliate program

2. Semrush

Strong fit for SEO, traffic growth and keyword-research content. Semrush currently advertises commissions from $100 to $300 per sale and $10 for eligible free trials, with a 120-day cookie window.

Official Semrush affiliate program

3. Kinsta

Relevant for website hosting and performance content. Kinsta currently advertises a one-time WordPress referral commission of $50–$500 plus 10% monthly recurring commission.

Official Kinsta affiliate program

4. Name.com

A useful fit for domain, hosting and website-startup content. Name.com advertises 20% commission on standard domains, 30% on WordPress Hosting and 50% on yearly hosting and SSL for qualifying new-customer purchases.

Official Name.com affiliate program

5. Hosting.com

Relevant to hosting comparison and website-launch content. Its current affiliate page advertises up to $300 per sale, with different commission levels depending on product and affiliate sales volume.

Official Hosting.com affiliate program

Finland-specific option

Hostaan is a particularly relevant local option for Finnish website owners. Its affiliate page advertises 20% commission on new orders and recurring commission on certain larger WordPress, SmartWP and SmartWoo plans. The page also says the program can be joined without a company, although commissions may need to be invoiced through an entrepreneurship/cooperative service.

Official Hostaan affiliate program

What we should promote first

  1. SEO articles: Semrush.
  2. Website-building articles: Webflow and Name.com.
  3. Hosting articles: Kinsta and Hosting.com.
  4. Finnish-language/local content: Hostaan.

Important: we have not joined these programs yet

This page is a research shortlist, not a claim that MonetizeStack is already approved by any of these programs. Before using tracked affiliate links, we need to apply, receive approval and replace the normal links with the program's actual tracking URLs.

€0 execution plan

We do not need to buy ads or software. First publish commercial-intent guides, add clear calls to action, apply to the most relevant programs, and track which pages generate outbound clicks. Only after we see real clicks should we spend time expanding the partner portfolio.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on MonetizeStack may become affiliate links. If a reader purchases through an approved affiliate link, MonetizeStack may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Recommendations should be based on relevance and usefulness, not commission alone.
