Choose the deliverable first

Instead of asking for “a better website,” define one page, one screen set or one design problem. Give the freelancer your audience, goal and required sections. This makes offers easier to compare.

Budget tip: start with a single high-value page such as a homepage or landing page. If the result is strong, expand the relationship rather than paying for a complete redesign immediately.

What to check in a Fiverr profile

Review recent work that resembles your project, read reviews for evidence of communication and delivery, and make sure the service includes the file format or implementation you actually need. A beautiful portfolio is not enough if the freelancer cannot meet your technical requirements.
