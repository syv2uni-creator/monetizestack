Useful copywriting projects

  • Homepage and landing-page copy.
  • Product and service descriptions.
  • Email sequences and promotional copy.
  • Calls to action and conversion-focused rewrites.
  • Website content editing and clarity improvements.

Give the writer a strong brief

Explain who the page is for, what you sell, the main action you want visitors to take, the tone you want and the facts that must be included. Share examples of competitors or brands you like when useful, but do not ask a freelancer to copy another site's text.

Start with one important page.
A homepage or revenue-focused landing page is easier to review than a large batch of articles. Measure whether the new copy improves clarity, clicks, leads or sales before expanding the project.

SEO copywriting warning

Do not buy copy simply because a seller promises a certain keyword density or guaranteed rankings. Search visibility depends on much more than word count. Useful content should serve readers first.

How to compare offers

Compare the actual deliverables, revision policy, research depth, language quality and relevant portfolio examples. The cheapest offer is not necessarily the lowest-cost option if the copy requires a complete rewrite.
