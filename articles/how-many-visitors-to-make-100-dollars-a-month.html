How Many Website Visitors Do You Need to Make $100 a Month?

There is no fixed visitor count for $100 a month. The answer depends on pageviews, advertising RPM, affiliate clicks, conversion rate and commission per sale.

Simple ad example: at a $10 page RPM, you need about 10,000 pageviews to generate $100. At a $20 RPM, about 5,000 pageviews. Visitors are not the same as pageviews.

Affiliate Example

If 1,000 monthly visitors generate 100 affiliate clicks, 3% of those clicks convert, and the average commission is $35, the site would generate about $105 in estimated gross commission. This is an illustration, not a guarantee.

What Traffic Level Should You Target?

For ads, 5,000–15,000 monthly pageviews can be enough to cross $100 under some RPM assumptions. For affiliate marketing, a much smaller audience can work if the visitors have strong purchase intent and the offer is closely matched.

How to Reach $100 Faster

  • Target specific problems instead of broad topics.
  • Create pages with commercial intent as well as informational content.
  • Link useful guides to relevant calculators.
  • Test affiliate offers before relying on display ads alone.
  • Measure revenue per visitor, not just traffic.

Bottom Line

There is no magic traffic number. $100 per month can require thousands of ad-supported pageviews or only a few well-qualified affiliate conversions. Monetization quality matters as much as traffic volume.
