What Is Website RPM?

Website RPM is one of the simplest ways to measure how effectively a website turns pageviews into revenue. Here's what RPM means, how to calculate it and what website owners can do to improve it.

What does RPM mean?

RPM stands for Revenue Per Mille. "Mille" means one thousand, so RPM represents the amount of revenue generated for every 1,000 pageviews.

Website owners commonly use RPM to understand how well their traffic is being monetized. Instead of looking only at total revenue, RPM allows you to compare monetization performance across different amounts of traffic.

How do you calculate website RPM?

The basic formula is:

RPM = Revenue ÷ Pageviews × 1,000

For example, imagine a website earns $600 from 50,000 pageviews.

$600 ÷ 50,000 × 1,000 = $12 RPM

That means the website generated approximately $12 for every 1,000 pageviews during that period.

Why is RPM important?

Traffic is important, but traffic alone does not determine how much money a website makes.

Two websites can have the same number of visitors but generate very different amounts of revenue. RPM helps explain part of that difference.

If your traffic stays constant while your RPM increases, your website can generate more revenue without needing additional visitors.

What affects website RPM?

Website RPM can vary considerably between websites. Some of the most important factors include:

  • Audience location: Advertiser demand differs between countries and regions.
  • Niche: Some topics attract higher-value advertisers.
  • Traffic source: Search, social and direct visitors can behave differently.
  • Device: Desktop and mobile traffic can produce different monetization results.
  • Ad placements: The number and position of advertisements can influence revenue.
  • Seasonality: Advertising demand can change throughout the year.

Page RPM vs session RPM

RPM can be calculated using different units. Page RPM usually measures revenue per 1,000 pageviews, while session RPM measures revenue per 1,000 sessions.

These metrics answer slightly different questions, so it is important to understand which RPM definition a platform or report is using.

How can you increase website RPM?

Increasing RPM generally means generating more revenue from the traffic you already have.

Possible strategies include improving ad placements, testing different monetization methods, improving content quality and attracting higher-value traffic.

Affiliate marketing, digital products and lead generation can also increase revenue per visitor without relying entirely on advertising.

RPM is only one part of website monetization

RPM is useful because it gives website owners a simple metric for comparing monetization performance. However, it should not be treated as the only measure of a successful website.

A strong monetization strategy can combine advertising, affiliate marketing, products, subscriptions, sponsorships and other revenue sources.

Key takeaway: RPM tells you how much revenue your website generates per 1,000 pageviews. Tracking RPM over time can help you understand whether your monetization strategy is improving.
