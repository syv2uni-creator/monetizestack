How Many Website Visitors Do You Need to Make $500 a Month?

Reaching $500 per month depends less on a magic traffic number and more on how effectively the site monetizes each qualified visitor.

Ad example: at a $15 page RPM, $500 requires about 33,334 pageviews per month. At a $25 RPM, it requires about 20,000 pageviews.

Affiliate Example

Suppose 5,000 visitors generate 500 affiliate clicks. At a 2% conversion rate and a $50 average commission, estimated gross commission is $500. That is why purchase intent can dramatically reduce the traffic needed.

Traffic vs. Revenue Quality

A focused audience researching software, hosting, SEO or professional services can monetize differently from broad entertainment traffic. Commercial intent should be part of your content strategy.

A Practical Route to $500

  • Build around a narrow audience and clear problems.
  • Identify pages that attract buying intent.
  • Add useful calculators and comparison content.
  • Test relevant affiliate offers.
  • Use ads as a complementary revenue stream.
