How Much Does a Website With 10,000 Visitors Make?

10,000 monthly visitors is a useful milestone for a growing website. At this level, monetization starts to become measurable, but the result can range from very little to meaningful side income depending on the business model.

Example: if 10,000 visitors generate 15,000 pageviews and the site earns a $15 RPM, advertising revenue would be about $225 per month. At a $25 RPM, it would be about $375.

What About Affiliate Revenue?

Suppose 10,000 visitors generate 1,000 affiliate clicks. At a 3% conversion rate, that is 30 conversions. With a $25 average commission, the estimated gross commission is $750 per month.

This is an illustration, not a promise. Some sites will have much lower click-through and conversion rates, while highly commercial audiences can perform better.

How to Make 10,000 Visitors More Valuable

  • Separate informational traffic from commercial-intent traffic.
  • Build internal links from informational guides to relevant tools and offers.
  • Improve the pages that already attract qualified visitors.
  • Test affiliate offers where the product solves the visitor's problem.
  • Use advertising as one revenue stream rather than the entire business model.

Bottom Line
