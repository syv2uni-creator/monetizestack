How to Calculate Affiliate Revenue

Learn how to estimate affiliate revenue using clicks, conversion rate and average commission per conversion.

Affiliate revenue is the commission a website, creator or publisher earns when visitors complete a qualifying action through an affiliate link.

The calculation can be simple when you know how many affiliate clicks you generate, what percentage of those clicks convert and how much you earn from each conversion.

The Affiliate Revenue Formula

A simple way to estimate monthly affiliate revenue is to multiply monthly affiliate clicks by the conversion rate and the average commission earned per conversion.

Estimated Affiliate Revenue = Monthly Clicks × Conversion Rate × Average Commission

Because conversion rate is normally expressed as a percentage, divide it by 100 when performing the calculation.

Revenue = Clicks × (Conversion Rate ÷ 100) × Commission

Example: 1,000 Affiliate Clicks

Example calculation

Monthly affiliate clicks: 1,000

Conversion rate: 3%

Average commission: $25

Estimated conversions: 1,000 × 3% = 30

Estimated affiliate revenue: 30 × $25 = $750

With these assumptions, the website could generate approximately $750 per month in affiliate commissions.

What Is Affiliate Conversion Rate?

Affiliate conversion rate measures the percentage of tracked affiliate clicks that result in a qualifying conversion.

Conversion Rate = Conversions ÷ Affiliate Clicks × 100

For example, if 2,000 people click an affiliate link and 60 complete the qualifying action, the conversion rate is 3%.

It is important to use the conversion definition provided by the affiliate program. A conversion might be a sale, lead, signup or another qualifying action depending on the program.

How Commission Per Sale Works

Affiliate programs can use different payout structures. Some pay a percentage of an order, while others offer a fixed amount for a qualifying conversion.

Percentage-Based Commission

With a percentage-based program, commission is calculated from the attributed order value.

Commission Per Sale = Order Value × Commission Rate

Example

Average order value: $100

Commission rate: 20%

Commission per sale: $100 × 20% = $20

Fixed Commission

Some affiliate programs pay a fixed amount for every qualifying conversion. In that case, the commission per conversion is simply the stated payout.

Affiliate Revenue = Conversions × Fixed Commission

How Many Clicks Do You Need to Make $100?

You can also work backwards from a revenue goal.

Suppose your average commission is $25 and your conversion rate is 2%.

To earn $100, you need four conversions because $100 ÷ $25 = 4.

At a 2% conversion rate, approximately 200 affiliate clicks would be required to generate four conversions on average.

Required Clicks = Target Revenue ÷ Commission ÷ Conversion Rate

What Affects Affiliate Revenue?

Affiliate revenue can change significantly even when website traffic stays the same.

Traffic Quality

Visitors who are actively researching a product or service can behave differently from visitors who are simply browsing informational content.

Affiliate Click-Through Rate

More relevant clicks create more opportunities for conversions. Content placement, user intent and the relevance of the recommendation can all influence clicks.

Conversion Rate

A higher conversion rate means more conversions from the same number of affiliate clicks.

Commission

Two affiliate programs can produce very different revenue from the same number of conversions because their payouts can differ.

Offer Relevance

Affiliate recommendations generally work best when they are closely related to the visitor's needs and the subject of the content.

Affiliate Revenue vs. Advertising Revenue

Advertising and affiliate marketing monetize traffic in different ways.

Advertising revenue is commonly connected to impressions or pageviews, while affiliate revenue depends on qualifying actions such as purchases, leads or signups.

This means affiliate marketing can sometimes be valuable for websites with smaller but highly targeted audiences.

How to Increase Affiliate Revenue

There are several ways to improve affiliate revenue without relying only on more website traffic.

  • Create content that matches strong purchase intent.
  • Recommend products or services that are relevant to the audience.
  • Improve the visibility and placement of useful affiliate links.
  • Test different offers and merchants.
  • Track clicks and conversions separately.
  • Compare earnings per click between offers.
  • Improve content quality and visitor trust.

Why Affiliate Revenue Estimates Are Not Guarantees

A calculator provides an estimate based on the assumptions you enter. Actual affiliate payouts can differ because programs have their own attribution rules and qualifying conditions.

Refunds, cancellations, attribution windows, program terms and other adjustments can affect the final amount that is actually paid.

For that reason, it is useful to compare several scenarios rather than relying on a single forecast.

Final Thoughts

Affiliate revenue can be estimated with a simple formula:

Clicks × Conversion Rate × Commission

The most important inputs are the number of affiliate clicks, the percentage of those clicks that convert and the average commission generated by each conversion.

Once you know those numbers, you can estimate potential monthly revenue and identify which part of the funnel offers the biggest opportunity for improvement.
