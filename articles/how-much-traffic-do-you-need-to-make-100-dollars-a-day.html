How Much Traffic Do You Need to Make $100 a Day?

$100 a day is about $3,000 a month. The traffic target changes dramatically depending on RPM and pages per visitor.

Example: at a $10 RPM and 2 pages per visitor, you need about 5,000 visitors a day to target $100/day in estimated ad revenue.

How to approach a $100/day target

At this level, relying on a single revenue source can be limiting. Build qualified traffic, improve the value of each pageview and add relevant monetization such as affiliate marketing where appropriate.

For example, a website could combine advertising with useful recommendations for SEO, design, content or technical work. The commercial recommendation should always match the reader's actual problem.
