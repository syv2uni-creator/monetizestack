How Much Traffic Do You Need to Make $10 a Day?

$10 a day is about $300 a month. Your required traffic depends on RPM and how many pages each visitor views.

Example: at a $10 RPM and 2 pages per visitor, you need about 500 visitors a day to target $10/day in estimated ad revenue.

How to reach $10/day

Focus on qualified search traffic, useful content, internal links and a healthy RPM. You can also add relevant affiliate monetization where it genuinely helps the reader.

What if traffic is your bottleneck?

If your numbers show that you need more visitors, identify the specific problem before paying for help. SEO, content strategy, technical SEO or WordPress work can be useful when they match the actual bottleneck.
