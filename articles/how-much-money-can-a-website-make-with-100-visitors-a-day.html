How Much Money Can a Website Make With 100 Visitors a Day?

100 visitors a day is about 3,000 visitors a month. What that traffic is worth depends on pageviews, RPM and any additional monetization.

Quick estimate: with 2 pages per visitor and a $10 RPM, 100 visitors a day could produce about $60/month in ad revenue.

Why 100 visitors a day is only the beginning

At this traffic level, improving monetization can matter as much as acquiring more visitors. Better internal linking, useful content, stronger RPM and relevant affiliate offers can increase the value of the same audience.
