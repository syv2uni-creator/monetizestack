How Much Money Can a Website Make With 1,000 Visitors a Day?

1,000 visitors a day is roughly 30,000 visitors a month. The revenue potential depends on pageviews, RPM and the monetization mix.

Quick estimate: at 2 pages per visitor and a $10 RPM, 1,000 visitors a day could produce about $600/month in estimated ad revenue.

Can 1,000 daily visitors make $1,000 a month?

Yes, the arithmetic can work at the right RPM and pageview level. At 2 pages per visitor, an RPM of about $16.67 would produce roughly $1,000 from 60,000 monthly pageviews. Actual results vary by audience, niche and monetization setup.

Look beyond ads

Once traffic is meaningful, affiliate offers can complement advertising. The best approach is to match a commercial recommendation to a genuine user problem instead of adding unrelated links.
