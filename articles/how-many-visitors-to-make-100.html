How Many Visitors Do You Need to Make $100?

The number of visitors you need to make $100 depends on how your website is monetized, how many pageviews each visitor generates and how much revenue you earn per 1,000 pageviews.

The short answer

There is no universal number of visitors required to make $100 from a website.

A website earning a $5 page RPM may need significantly more traffic than a website earning a $20 RPM.

This is why it is useful to look at both traffic and monetization performance.

How do you calculate the required traffic?

If you are estimating advertising revenue, you can start with the RPM formula.

Revenue = (Pageviews ÷ 1,000) × RPM

To find the number of pageviews needed for $100, the formula can be rearranged:

Required Pageviews = ($100 ÷ RPM) × 1,000

How many pageviews are needed for $100?

Here are some example scenarios:

RPM Pageviews Needed
$2 50,000
$5 20,000
$10 10,000
$15 6,667
$20 5,000
$25 4,000
These are mathematical examples, not guaranteed advertising rates. Actual RPM can vary substantially between websites and audiences.

Visitors are not the same as pageviews

One of the most important distinctions is that visitors and pageviews are different metrics.

If one visitor views only one page, 10,000 visitors could produce approximately 10,000 pageviews.

But if the average visitor views two pages, 10,000 visitors could produce approximately 20,000 pageviews.

That difference can have a major effect on advertising revenue.

Example: $100 with a $10 RPM

Suppose your website has an RPM of $10.

To generate $100:

($100 ÷ $10) × 1,000 = 10,000 pageviews

If your average visitor views two pages, you would need approximately:

10,000 pageviews ÷ 2 pages per visitor = 5,000 visitors

In this example, approximately 5,000 visitors could produce the 10,000 pageviews required for $100 at a $10 RPM.

What if you use affiliate marketing?

Advertising is not the only way to make $100 from a website.

Affiliate marketing works differently because revenue depends on clicks, conversions and commissions rather than simply pageviews.

For example, a website could potentially generate $100 from a relatively small amount of highly targeted traffic if visitors are interested in products or services with meaningful commissions.

On the other hand, a website relying entirely on display advertising may need substantially more pageviews.

How can you reach $100 faster?

There are two basic levers you can improve:

  • Increase the amount of relevant traffic.
  • Increase the revenue generated from existing traffic.

Increasing traffic can involve publishing useful content, targeting relevant search queries and building distribution channels.

Increasing monetization can involve improving RPM, testing different monetization methods and adding relevant affiliate offers or products.

The important lesson

You do not necessarily need hundreds of thousands of visitors to make your first $100 online.

What matters is the relationship between traffic, pageviews, monetization and audience intent.

For an advertising-focused website, RPM is especially important because it tells you how efficiently your pageviews are being monetized.

Key takeaway: The number of visitors required to make $100 depends on your monetization model. For advertising, start by estimating the required pageviews from your RPM and then convert pageviews into visitors using your average pages per visitor.
