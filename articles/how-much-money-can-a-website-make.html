How Much Money Can a Website Make?

A website can make anything from a few dollars per month to a substantial online business. The difference usually comes down to traffic, audience quality, niche and how effectively the website is monetized.

The short answer

There is no single average amount that every website earns. A small website with a few thousand monthly pageviews may generate only a modest amount from advertising, while a larger website can combine advertising, affiliate commissions, products and other revenue streams.

Even two websites with the same amount of traffic can produce very different revenue.

What determines how much a website makes?

The most important factors are:

  • Traffic: More qualified visitors generally create more monetization opportunities.
  • Pageviews: A visitor who views several pages can create more advertising opportunities than a visitor who views only one page.
  • Niche: Advertiser demand and affiliate opportunities vary significantly between industries.
  • Audience: Visitor location, interests and purchasing intent can influence revenue.
  • Monetization model: Advertising is only one possible revenue source.
  • Conversion rate: Affiliate links, products and services depend on how many visitors take an action.

How much can website advertising make?

Advertising revenue is often estimated using RPM, or revenue per 1,000 pageviews.

The basic calculation is:

Estimated Revenue = (Pageviews ÷ 1,000) × RPM

For example, a website receiving 50,000 pageviews per month with a $10 RPM would generate an estimated:

50,000 ÷ 1,000 × $10 = $500

This is only an estimate. Actual advertising revenue can vary based on factors such as audience location, niche, device, seasonality, ad placement and the advertising network.

Websites can make money without relying only on ads

Advertising is one monetization method, but it does not have to be the only one.

A website can combine several revenue streams, including:

  • Display advertising
  • Affiliate marketing
  • Digital products
  • Paid memberships
  • Sponsorships
  • Lead generation
  • Services and consulting

In many cases, combining multiple revenue streams can make a website less dependent on a single source of income.

How many visitors do you need to make money?

There is no fixed traffic requirement for making money. A small website can generate revenue if its audience has strong commercial intent or if the website promotes high-value products and services.

On the other hand, a website that relies mainly on display advertising usually needs considerably more traffic before advertising revenue becomes meaningful.

This is why the number of visitors alone is not enough to estimate website income.

How can you increase website revenue?

There are two broad ways to increase website revenue: attract more valuable traffic or earn more from the traffic you already have.

Improving content, targeting better keywords, increasing pageviews per visitor and testing monetization methods can all contribute to higher revenue.

For advertising-focused websites, improving RPM can be especially useful because revenue can increase without requiring the same proportional increase in traffic.

The real goal is not just more traffic

Traffic is valuable only when it can be converted into revenue, leads, customers or another meaningful outcome.

A smaller website with a highly relevant audience can sometimes be more valuable than a much larger website with low-intent traffic.

The best strategy is therefore to build useful content, attract the right audience and create several sensible ways for that audience to generate value.

Key takeaway: There is no universal amount that a website can make. Traffic, RPM, niche, audience quality and monetization strategy all matter. Use revenue estimates as planning tools rather than promises.
