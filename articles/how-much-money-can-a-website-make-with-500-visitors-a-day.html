How Much Money Can a Website Make With 500 Visitors a Day?

500 visitors a day is roughly 15,000 visitors a month. At this scale, small improvements in RPM and pages per visitor can become meaningful.

Quick estimate: at 2 pages per visitor and a $10 RPM, 500 visitors a day could produce about $300/month in estimated ad revenue.

How to move toward $1,000/month

At 500 daily visitors, the fastest improvement may come from combining traffic growth with better monetization. Test content topics, internal links, RPM improvements and relevant affiliate offers rather than assuming more traffic is the only answer.
