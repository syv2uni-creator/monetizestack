How the calculation works

Affiliate revenue depends on the number of affiliate clicks, the percentage of clicks that convert and the average commission earned from each conversion.

Estimated Revenue = Affiliate Clicks × Conversion Rate × Average Commission

For example, 1,000 monthly clicks with a 3% conversion rate would produce approximately 30 conversions. At an average $25 commission, the estimated revenue would be $750 per month.

This calculator provides an estimate. Actual affiliate revenue can vary depending on product demand, traffic quality, commission structures, refunds and conversion performance.
