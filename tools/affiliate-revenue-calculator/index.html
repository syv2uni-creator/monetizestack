How the Affiliate Revenue Calculator Works

Affiliate revenue is estimated from three inputs: affiliate clicks, conversion rate and average commission per conversion.

Estimated revenue = affiliate clicks × (conversion rate ÷ 100) × average commission

For example, 1,000 clicks at a 3% conversion rate produce 30 expected conversions. At a $25 average commission, that equals an estimated $750 per month or $9,000 per year.

What Can Increase Affiliate Revenue?

There are three main levers: more qualified clicks, a higher conversion rate, and higher average commissions. Better commercial content, stronger calls to action and better-matched offers can improve performance without necessarily requiring more traffic.

Why Conversion Rate Matters

A small change in conversion rate can materially affect earnings. For example, moving from 2% to 3% conversion produces 50% more conversions from the same number of clicks. Actual conversion rates vary widely by niche, offer, traffic intent and audience.

Affiliate Revenue vs. Ad Revenue

Advertising usually monetizes pageviews, while affiliate marketing monetizes actions such as clicks and purchases. A site with relatively low traffic but strong commercial intent can sometimes earn more from affiliate offers than from display advertising.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good affiliate conversion rate?

There is no universal benchmark. Conversion depends on the offer, audience, traffic intent, device mix and how closely the product matches the visitor's needs.

Can affiliate revenue be higher than ad revenue?

Yes. Affiliate revenue can be substantially higher per visitor when visitors have strong purchase intent and the offer pays meaningful commissions.

Does this calculator include refunds?

No. It estimates gross expected commission. Refunds, cancellations, rejected conversions and program-specific rules can reduce actual payouts.
