How the Website Revenue Calculator Works

First, monthly pageviews are estimated by multiplying monthly visitors by pages per visitor. Revenue is then estimated from the RPM you enter.

Monthly revenue = (monthly visitors × pages per visitor ÷ 1,000) × RPM

For example, 25,000 visitors × 2.2 pages per visitor equals 55,000 monthly pageviews. At a $12 RPM, estimated revenue is $660 per month or $7,920 per year.

Why RPM Matters

RPM can vary substantially by niche, audience location, device mix, seasonality, traffic quality, ad demand and monetization setup. Use the scenario cards as planning ranges rather than guaranteed earnings.

How to Increase Website Revenue

You can generally grow revenue by increasing qualified traffic, improving pages per visitor, increasing RPM, or adding additional monetization methods such as affiliate marketing. Use the calculator to compare these changes before making decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is website RPM?

RPM is estimated revenue per 1,000 pageviews. It is commonly used to compare website monetization performance.

Is this calculator accurate?

It provides a mathematical estimate based on the numbers you enter. Actual revenue can be higher or lower because RPM and traffic quality change over time.

What is a good RPM?

There is no universal good RPM. Results depend heavily on niche, geography, audience, seasonality and monetization method.
