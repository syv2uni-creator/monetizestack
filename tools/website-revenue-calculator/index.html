How the Website Revenue Calculator Works

Monthly pageviews are estimated by multiplying monthly visitors by pages per visitor.

Estimated revenue is then calculated by dividing monthly pageviews by 1,000 and multiplying the result by your estimated RPM.

For example, 25,000 visitors × 2.2 pages per visitor equals 55,000 monthly pageviews. At a $12 RPM, estimated revenue is $660 per month.
