How does the calculation work?

The calculator estimates your monthly pageviews first, then applies your estimated Page RPM.

Monthly Revenue = (Visitors × Pages Per Visitor ÷ 1,000) × RPM

For example, 25,000 visitors viewing 2.2 pages each produces approximately 55,000 monthly pageviews. At a $12 RPM, the estimated monthly revenue would be $660.

Actual revenue can vary significantly depending on traffic source, audience location, device type, ad placements, niche and monetization method.
