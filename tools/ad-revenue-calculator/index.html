How the calculation works

Ad revenue is commonly estimated using pageviews and RPM, which represents estimated revenue earned for every 1,000 pageviews.

The calculator divides your monthly pageviews by 1,000 and multiplies the result by your estimated advertising RPM.

Estimated Ad Revenue = (Monthly Pageviews ÷ 1,000) × Ad RPM

For example, 55,000 monthly pageviews at a $12 RPM would produce an estimated $660 in monthly advertising revenue.
