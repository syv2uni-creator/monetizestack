How the Ad Revenue Calculator Works

The calculator estimates advertising revenue by dividing monthly pageviews by 1,000 and multiplying the result by your estimated ad RPM.

Estimated ad revenue = (monthly pageviews ÷ 1,000) × ad RPM

Example

55,000 monthly pageviews at a $12 ad RPM produce an estimated $660 in monthly ad revenue, or $7,920 over a full year if the same performance continues.

What Is Ad RPM?

Ad RPM is a publisher-side revenue metric that estimates how much advertising revenue is generated per 1,000 pageviews. It is different from advertiser CPM, which generally describes advertiser cost per 1,000 ad impressions.

What Affects Ad Revenue?

Actual advertising revenue can vary with audience geography, niche, device mix, seasonality, ad demand, viewability, ad placements, traffic quality and the advertising platform being used.

How to Increase Ad Revenue

There are two broad levers: increase qualified pageviews or improve the revenue generated from existing pageviews. Better RPM can come from stronger ad demand, audience mix, placements, viewability and broader monetization optimization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ad revenue the same as total website revenue?

No. A website can earn from advertising as well as affiliate commissions, sponsorships, products, subscriptions and other sources.

Is a higher RPM always better?

A higher RPM generally means more revenue per pageview, but changes should be evaluated alongside user experience, traffic quality and long-term performance.

Are these ad revenue estimates guaranteed?

No. They are planning estimates based on the inputs you provide. Actual advertising earnings fluctuate over time.
