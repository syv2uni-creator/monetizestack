How the calculation works

Future traffic is estimated by applying the monthly growth rate repeatedly for the selected number of months.

Future Traffic = Current Traffic × (1 + Growth Rate) ^ Months

For example, 25,000 monthly visitors growing by 5% every month would reach approximately 44,866 monthly visitors after 12 months.

This is a projection rather than a guarantee. Actual website traffic can vary because of SEO, seasonality, content changes, competition and other factors.
