What Is Website RPM?

RPM stands for revenue per mille, meaning revenue per 1,000 pageviews. It is a useful metric for comparing website monetization performance even when sites have different traffic volumes.

RPM = Revenue ÷ Pageviews × 1,000

Example

If a website earns $660 from 55,000 pageviews, its RPM is $12. That means the site generated an estimated $12 for every 1,000 pageviews during that period.

Why Does RPM Matter?

If traffic stays constant while RPM increases, revenue increases without requiring additional pageviews. RPM can therefore help website owners evaluate ad optimization, audience quality and other monetization changes.

What Affects Website RPM?

RPM can vary by niche, audience geography, device mix, traffic source, seasonality, ad demand, ad placements and monetization method. There is no universal RPM that every website should expect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RPM the same as CPM?

No. CPM generally refers to advertiser cost per 1,000 ad impressions, while RPM is a publisher-side revenue metric and can be based on pageviews or another defined unit.

What is a good website RPM?

There is no single benchmark that applies to every site. Compare your RPM over time and against comparable sites with similar audiences and monetization models.

Can I use this calculator for monthly revenue?

Yes. Enter your revenue and pageviews from the same month or other matching period. The calculator converts the result to revenue per 1,000 pageviews.
