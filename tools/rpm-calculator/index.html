What is website RPM?

RPM stands for revenue per mille. In website monetization, RPM is commonly used to describe how much revenue a website generates per 1,000 pageviews.

RPM = Revenue ÷ Pageviews × 1,000

Example

If your website earns $660 from 55,000 pageviews, your RPM is $12.

RPM can vary significantly depending on your audience, traffic source, geographic location, niche, device mix, ad placements and monetization method.

Why does RPM matter?

RPM gives website owners a simple way to compare monetization performance over time. If your traffic stays the same but your RPM increases, your website is generating more revenue from the same amount of traffic.
